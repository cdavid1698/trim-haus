# Business profile — Trim Haus Gents Salon

*Single source of truth for site content. Fact refs (F#) point to `sources.md`.*

## Who they are
Trim Haus is a men's barbershop on Khalifa Street in Al Ain's Central District (Hai Qesaidah), open since 2022 (F5, F10). It calls itself **"The Filipino Barbershop"** (F2): a team of Filipino barbers in crisp white mandarin-collar tunics with the gold TH monogram embroidered on the chest, working in black gloves (F14). Signage is bilingual English/Arabic — "صالون تريم هاوس للرجال" (F1). It's open every day, 9 am – 10 pm (F6).

## What they sell
Straightforward, affordable grooming with a clear printed price list (F9, AED):

- **Cuts & shaves:** Haircut 25 · Haircut & Shave 35 · Regular Shave 15
- **Care:** Basic Facial 15 · Shampoo 10 · Scalp Massage 30 · Hair Spa 30
- **Colour:** Basic Colour 40 · Highlights 50
- **Combos:** Haircut + Shampoo 30 · Haircut + Facial 35 · Haircut + Facial + Shampoo 40 · Haircut + Basic Colour 60 · Haircut + Scalp Massage 50 · Haircut + Hair Spa 50

Booking today = phone call / walk-in / Facebook. No online booking, no website (F16).

## Who buys
Working men in Al Ain's Central District — a large Filipino community plus Arab and South Asian residents (photos and reviews show a mixed clientele, F11). Customers want a sharp fade or shape-up (F15, F11) at a fair price, without a wait, often after work (open till 10 pm). Language: English first, Arabic second.

## How they differ
- **Identity:** proudly Filipino barbers — a community draw (they sponsor a basketball team, "Team TRIM HAUS x PRIMO", F13).
- **Price:** AED 25 haircut vs AED 40 at Evano Spa (competitors.md).
- **Presentation:** uniforms, gloves, gold branding and a professional photo shoot — looks more premium than its price.
- **Skill reputation:** fades, named barber Jo Mar praised in reviews (F11).
